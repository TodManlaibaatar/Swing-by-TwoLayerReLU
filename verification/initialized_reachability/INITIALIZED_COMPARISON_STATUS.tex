\section{Initialized continuation and the failed entry comparison}
The initialized reversal theorem remains open. Starting at the exact
archived parameter vector, the interval verifier establishes a tube through
$4974$ intervals of length $1/5000$, hence through $t=0.9948$.
The initial comparison radius is exactly zero. Every step satisfies its
strict tube inequality, including all compatible training-gate changes.
All smooth-rate coefficients were independently recomputed, and the
accumulation was replayed from zero with $60$-digit interval exponentials.

Let $\bar r_j$ be the outward-rounded comparison radius, $k_j\ge0$ its
one-sided rate, and $\omega_j\ge0$ the sum of the reference-defect and
adverse-gate intercept bounds. The checked recurrence is
\[
 \bar r_j=\operatorname{up}\left[
 e^{k_j/5000}\bar r_{j-1}
 +\frac{e^{k_j/5000}-1}{k_j}\omega_j\right].
\]
At the first failure of the \emph{entry-budget} comparison, the ledger gives
\begin{align*}
 j&=4974, &t_{j-1}&=0.9946,&t_j&=0.9948,\\
 \bar r_{j-1}&\approx1.1982855683396031\cdot10^{-5},&&&\\
 \bar r_j&\approx1.2003084494454761\cdot10^{-5}
                  >1.2\cdot10^{-5}.&&&
\end{align*}
Here $k_j<4.709629$, $\omega_j<4.466164\cdot10^{-5}$.
The prescribed tube radius is greater than $1.5425235\cdot10^{-5}$;
its strict slack is greater than $3.4221508\cdot10^{-6}$.
Thus the actual tube criterion has \emph{not} failed. The failed sufficient
inequality is specifically
\begin{equation}\label{initcert:failed-budget}
 \boxed{\bar r_{4974}\le1.2\cdot10^{-5}.}
\end{equation}
The exact binary upper bounds and rational step are recorded in
\texttt{INITIAL\_FLOW\_CERTIFICATE.json}; the full accepted prefix is in
\texttt{INITIAL\_FLOW\_ENCLOSURE.csv}.

This distinction is essential. The result proves
$\|\theta(t_j)-y(t_j)\|\le\bar r_j$, not a lower bound on the actual
error. The nondecreasing comparison used here cannot subsequently restore
the selected entry tolerance at $t=3.4$. A sharper propagation method,
a better-defect reference, a different sufficient entry set, or an
observable enclosure could still succeed. No structural obstruction to
initialized reversal, and no justification for a theorem-scope pivot,
follows from~\eqref{initcert:failed-budget}.

At this step the reference gate correction is less than
$4.068304\cdot10^{-5}$, while the smooth reference defect is less than
$1.611855\cdot10^{-6}$. The proof bottleneck is therefore not addressed
by checking only a smooth integrator's truncation error. A continuation
that improves this estimate must account for actual gate events and their
signed propagation. The isolated first-event proposition above does this
for one event; it does not certify the entire event sequence.

\paragraph{Independent arithmetic audit.}
A suspected normalization error proved to be a misreading of parentheses.
Both the certainly-active and uncertain residual contributions had exactly
one factor $1/N$. Independent recomputation agreed within
$1.8\cdot10^{-15}$ in the smooth rates; the delivered expression makes
that normalization explicit. No alleged correction to the underlying
mathematical bound is claimed.
